About Futures Trading

Futures contracts are agreements to buy or sell something, like oil or an index, at a set price on a future date. Beginners use futures to learn how prices change and how traders manage risk.

Key Beginner Concepts

  • Contract: A standardized agreement traded on an exchange.
  • Margin: The amount of money you must deposit to open a trade.
  • Leverage: Controlling more value with less money, which increases both gains and losses.
  • Expiration: The date when the contract ends.

Why Beginners Learn Futures

  • To understand how markets react to news and events.
  • To practice risk management with small positions.
  • To build confidence before trading larger amounts.

Beginner Tips

  • Start with education, not real money.
  • Use demo accounts when possible.
  • Focus on one or two markets at first.
