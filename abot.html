Our mission

Bright Path Wellness exists to make health information feel approachable and inclusive. The site is intentionally simple so that users with different devices, abilities, and backgrounds can access the content without barriers.

Core values

  1. Accessibility: Content is designed to be perceivable, operable, understandable, and robust.
  2. Clarity: Simple language and clear structure help users find what they need quickly.
  3. Respect: We assume users have different needs and design with empathy.

Who this site is for

This site is for busy students, working adults, and anyone who wants quick, practical wellness tips without ads, pop-ups, or confusing layouts.
