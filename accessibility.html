Accessibility Statement

This site is designed for beginners, and we also want it to be usable by as many people as possible, including those with disabilities.

How We Support Accessibility

  • Clear headings and simple language for new learners.
  • High‑contrast colors to improve readability.
  • Alt text on images to describe visuals.
  • Keyboard‑friendly navigation.
  • Responsive layout for phones, tablets, and desktops.

Contact About Accessibility

If you have trouble using this site or notice something that could be improved, please let us know through the Contact Us page.
